How to play Hungry Worm

👆

Drag anywhere to steer the worm. Drag far, or hold a second finger down, to boost. (Desktop: drag with the mouse, or use arrows / WASD, and hold Shift to boost.)

🐑

Burst out of the ground to snap up animals. Eat several in one leap for a combo!

🪱

The hunger bar drains. Keep eating or your worm flops over!

🥅

Nets and water sprays drain your hunger. Farmers always run away!
